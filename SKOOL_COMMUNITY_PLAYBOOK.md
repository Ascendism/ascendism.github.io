## 1. Purpose

This document translates proven Skool community-building tactics into a **Window of Ascent (WoA)** playbook focused on **permaculture practitioners, farmers, homesteaders, and land stewards** — not generic online-business or print-on-demand communities.

It answers:

- **Why** add Skool alongside Discord and the public site
- **Who** the paid community serves (narrow niche + transformation)
- **How** to onboard, retain, and compound member value
- **What** to steal from Skool playbook research and what to reject

## 3. Niche positioning (go narrow)

### Primary audience (eco-first)

From [`PLAN.md`](../PLAN.md) and CFP persona design:

> People who care about **material reality**: land, food, water, housing, work, energy, ownership, local resilience — and want a path toward greater autonomy using modern tools pragmatically.

**Skool community one-liner (draft):**

> *We help land stewards and regenerative growers build materially grounded, cooperative lives — using appropriate technology and peer support — before the window closes.*

### Transformation promise

Pick **one transformation**, not everything:

| ❌ Too broad | ✅ WoA narrow |
|------------|---------------|
| "Help everyone build community" | "Help first-time land seekers form a vetted 3–8 person cell with a 90-day action plan" |
| "Teach permaculture" | "Help small-acre stewards implement their first water + soil + food forest zone plan" |
| "AI for everyone" | "Help homesteaders deploy one offline-capable AI workflow for land planning or build logs" |

### Origin story frame (profession / pain / passion)

From Skool research short [-DyPsjIqkek](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/-DyPsjIqkek):

Communities succeed when rooted in **one of three**:

1. **Past profession** — e.g., builder, engineer, farmer who lived the problem
2. **Past pain** — e.g., failed land purchase, isolated homesteading, bureaucratic enclosure
3. **Passion** — e.g., regenerative systems, appropriate tech, cooperative land access

**WoA primary hook:** pain + passion — *"I watched 25k Discord members go silent because there was no structure, no transformation, no leadership"* (adapted from Adnan's Discord→Skool migration story) maps directly to building **intentional** land-steward community, not audience accumulation.

### Anti-pattern #1: teach what you don't know

Do **not** position WoA Skool as expert-led permaculture masterclass unless the teacher has lived it. Instead:

- **Curate** peer expertise (member showcases, guest experts)
- **Document** experiments (AI Versus Reality format from [`discordLandBot/COMMUNITY_EVENTS_DESIGN.md`](../discordLandBot/COMMUNITY_EVENTS_DESIGN.md))
- **Facilitate** land diligence and group formation (PMA educational container, not investment advice)

## 4. The 4 Cs — adapted for WoA

From Adnan's framework ([mf1WAw0FyaI](https://youtu.be/mf1WAw0FyaI)), adapted from POD business to regenerative land stewardship:

### Cause

**Mission:** Use the temporary technological window to help ordinary people build autonomous, resilient, materially grounded lives through cooperative land stewardship and appropriate technology.

**Not:** get-rich-quick, passive income, generic "community building."

### Culture

**Rewarded:**

- Build logs (soil tests, water catchment, guild plantings, appropriate-tech installs)
- Wins posted publicly (first harvest, first cell formed, first site walkthrough completed)
- Sourced research and honest experiment reports
- Helping another member get unstuck (peer mentorship)

**Not tolerated:**

- Investment solicitation or "buy this land deal" spam
- Doom without constructive angle
- Self-promotion unrelated to member benefit
- Teaching beyond lived experience without labeling it as theory/hypothesis

Document explicitly in Skool **#rules** and onboarding video; mirror in Discord [`pma/discord-structure.md`](../pma/discord-structure.md).

### Container

**Skool** = paid classroom + community feed + calendar + leaderboards (single login).

**Discord** = daily ops, voice lounges, regional cells, bot-driven events ([`discordLandBot/`](../discordLandBot/)).

**Website** = book, philosophy, application funnel, archive.

### Connection

Engineer member-to-member bonding:

- Introduction ritual (region + acreage + current project + one ask)
- Accountability pairs (land-search buddy, build partner)
- Regional rollup threads
- Homestead Guild events (see §7)

## 5. Discord → Skool dual-platform strategy

### Research insight

Adnan migrated a **25,000-member silent Discord** to an **invite-only paid Skool** community and rebuilt demand through clarity ([mf1WAw0FyaI](https://youtu.be/mf1WAw0FyaI)).

### WoA approach (not a wholesale Discord delete)

WoA should **not** delete Discord — it is already spec'd as operational hub. Instead:

| Phase | Discord | Skool |
|-------|---------|-------|
| **Launch** | Free applicant preview channels; member full server | Paid annual members; classroom + onboarding |
| **Growth** | Real-time events, voice, bot automation | Courses, challenges, wins wall, guest expert replays |
| **Scale** | Regional cells, land marketplace bot features | Tiered classrooms, guest expert library, case studies |

**Kill the noise principles still apply:**

- Invite-only paid Skool (no drive-by lurkers)
- Clear onboarding path (no "figure it out" landing)
- Vetted applicants via PMA pipeline ([`pma/membership-model.md`](../pma/membership-model.md))

## 7. Onboarding and retention

### Onboarding > content

From [mf1WAw0FyaI](https://youtu.be/mf1WAw0FyaI): best course fails if members don't know the next step. Churn dropped when onboarding was fixed (17% → 11% in source story).

### WoA 3-day activation path

| Day | Action |
|-----|--------|
| **Day 1** | Welcome post + 5-min video: "How this community works" + roadmap graphic + **quick win** (e.g., complete land-search profile worksheet or post intro in #introduce-yourself) |
| **Day 2** | DM check-in from mod/Cortex-assisted success script: "What are you working on this season?" |
| **Day 3** | Accountability nudge: assign to Homestead Guild intro event or 7-day challenge |

**Automations:**

- Inactive 48h → gentle DM (mirror Adnan's automated inactive outreach)
- Cancel attempt → personal DM: "What happened? How can we help?" (retention tactic from research)

### 7-day steward challenge (first two weeks)

From [dDW3QJ7IWYc](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/dDW3QJ7IWYc):

Run a **7-day challenge** for early wins:

| Day | Challenge |
|-----|-----------|
| 1 | Post introduction (region, land status, one goal) |
| 2 | Share one site constraint (water, soil, access, zoning) |
| 3 | Post one photo or map of your context |
| 4 | Comment on two other members' posts with useful feedback |
| 5 | Complete one micro-action (soil test order, rainfall calc, seed list) |
| 6 | Attend live Q&A or voice lounge |
| 7 | Post win + next 30-day goal |

### Recognition and rewards

- **Wins wall** (Skool category): first guild planting, first cell meeting, first build log, first guest expert session attended
- Leaderboard points for constructive engagement (not volume spam)
- Monthly spotlight: "Steward of the month" — member-centric, not creator-centric

## 8. Rituals — Homestead Guild edition

Map Skool "engagement engineering" to existing event formats ([`discordLandBot/COMMUNITY_EVENTS_DESIGN.md`](../discordLandBot/COMMUNITY_EVENTS_DESIGN.md)):

| Skool ritual (research) | WoA Homestead Guild equivalent |
|---------------------------|--------------------------------|
| Niche of the day | **Species/guild of the week** — curated plant guild or appropriate-tech pattern ready to act on |
| Live Q&A | **Permaculture Site Review Night** — bi-weekly; members submit photos/maps |
| Community challenges | **7-day steward challenge** + **Design Challenge Week** (chicken tractor, rainwater, etc.) |
| Wins wall | **Harvest & build wins** — first fruit, first swale, first cell formed |
| Cancel DMs | PMA retention script (human or semi-automated) |

**"Chew the food" principle:** do the research legwork for members — e.g., weekly curated list of land-listing evaluation checklist updates, seasonal planting notes for their region, or pre-digested zoning resource links. This is **consumable action media**, not passive video courses.

## 9. Guest expert strategy

From [Cxov72hAE9A](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/Cxov72hAE9A):

Once WoA has **100–500+ vetted members**, trade **community access** for **30-minute Q&A or co-recorded session** with higher-status practitioners:

| Expert type | Offer | WoA gives |
|-------------|-------|-----------|
| Established permaculture designer | Live site-review session | Access to engaged steward audience + recorded replay for Skool library |
| Regenerative farmer | "What I'd do differently" call | Structured Q&A + case study asset |
| Land-access attorney (educational) | AMA on diligence (not advice) | Platform + disclaimer framework |
| Appropriate-tech builder | Demo + Q&A | Build In Public Night format |

**Outreach template (draft):**

> "We've built an invite-only community of [N] land stewards and regenerative growers working on real projects. I'd love to give your work exposure to them via a 30-minute Q&A — we handle moderation, recording, and promotion. Interested?"

## 10. Content funnel

From [dDW3QJ7IWYc](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/dDW3QJ7IWYc):

1. **Pick one niche, one transformation** (§3)
2. **Create content across socials** — book excerpts, homestead build clips, land-search tips, appropriate-tech demos; stay in niche
3. **Funnel to paid Skool** with clear offer (annual steward membership)
4. **Structure inside** (§7)
5. **Retain** with challenges + recognition
6. **Social proof loop** — member wins → marketing content → more aligned members

**WoA content sources:**

- Book chapters and research companions ([`BOOK/`](../BOOK/))
- Media hub / livestreams (future)
- Discord event replays → Skool library
- Member showcases (with permission)

**Do not** post side-hustle or unrelated trending topics — destroys authority ([dDW3QJ7IWYc](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/dDW3QJ7IWYc)).

## 14. Metrics that matter

| Metric | Target (Phase 1) | Why |
|--------|------------------|-----|
| 7-day activation (% completing challenge) | >60% | Onboarding quality |
| 30-day retention | >80% | Culture fit |
| Posts per active member / week | >1 | Engagement engineering |
| Wins wall posts / month | Growing | Social proof |
| Guest expert sessions / quarter | ≥1 | Authority without fake expertise |
