Why Join?

🎯 Purpose-Driven

We're building practical skills and connections for a future where self-reliance isn't just a dream but a reality. Every member brings something valuable to share.

🤝 Complementary Skills

Our group matching system pairs people based on complementary strengths and weaknesses. Learn from others while teaching what you know.

📚 Shared Learning

Access curated resources, participate in discussions, and contribute to our growing knowledge base on homesteading, permaculture, and natural building.

How to Join

  1. Join the Discord server — Click the button above to enter our community hub
  2. Read the rules — Check out #start-here for our community guidelines
  3. Complete your profile — Fill out your skills, interests, and goals
  4. Get interviewed — An admin will schedule a brief chat to understand your background
  5. Join a working group — Based on your profile, you'll be matched with complementary members
  6. Start building — Share knowledge, collaborate on projects, and grow together
