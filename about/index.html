PhotoniX aka Bobby Dean

Creator of sustainable tech, music, and eco-innovations. Building towards an eco-sustainable village.

I build systems that help people coordinate around land, autonomy, and material reality. This isn't about hype or perfect aesthetics. It's about using the tools we have — before the window closes — to help ordinary people build resilient, cooperative, grounded lives.

The Window of Ascent matching interview grew from watching thousands of people express intent but fail to coordinate. Theory without action is comfortable. Action without coordination is isolated. This project bridges that gap.

> No formal resume.
> If you need an LLM to determine my value, we're not meant to do business.

What I do

I write, design systems, and build coordination tools. My focus is practical: land discovery, group formation, community matching, and the infrastructure needed to move from planning to action.

The Window of Ascent manuscript explores the underlying thesis. The matching interview is the applied work.
