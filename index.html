Built from real life Not a fantasy of escape.

People before property

A shared future has to work in ordinary life.

Buying acreage is a financial decision. Sharing a place is also a relationship decision. Ascendism starts with the part that real-estate sites cannot answer: who you are, what you need, what you can offer, and how you want to live.

The first local effort is taking shape in rural Missouri, led by founder Bobby Dean. See why this place matters to the work →
