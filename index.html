The goal

Get people together to buy land and build intentional communities

This is not a book landing page. This is a matching system for people who want to build ecovillages, regenerative homesteads, and land-based cooperative communities. The window is narrowing — let's use it.

How it works

Step 1

Complete the interview

8 sections covering your goals, values, and readiness: land preferences, wealth, health, education, homesteading, permaculture, social dynamics, and motivation.

Step 2

Get matched

We connect you with others who share aligned values, timelines, and location preferences. No startup pitch, no fantasy homestead content — just honest coordination.

Step 3

Form your cell

Build a vetted 3–8 person group. Use our Discord for coordination, land research tools, and peer support as you move from planning to action.

The matching interview

This interview helps us understand your goals, resources, and readiness. Be honest — this is about finding real matches, not performing an ideal.

Section 1: Land & Location — Where do you want to be?

Climate zones, proximity to urban centers, existing property ownership, move timeline, land size preferences, and climate resilience priorities.

Questions cover: preferred regions, climate considerations, timeline to move, land size needs, proximity to cities, and existing land ownership.

Section 2: Wealth & Resources — What can you contribute?

Financial capacity, income stability, debt, investment readiness, and willingness to pool resources with others.

Questions cover: savings, income sources, debt load, investment capacity, and comfort with shared financial structures.

Section 3: Health & Physical Capacity — What can you do?

Physical capability for land work, dietary needs, healthcare access requirements, and disability accommodations.

Questions cover: physical work capacity, health conditions, dietary restrictions, and accessibility needs.

Section 4: Education & Skills — What do you know?

Formal training, practical skills, trades, teaching ability, and willingness to learn.

Questions cover: education background, practical skills (building, electrical, plumbing, mechanics), teaching experience, and learning commitment.

Section 5: Homesteading Experience — Have you done this?

Gardening, animal husbandry, food preservation, energy systems, water management, and off-grid living experience.

Questions cover: gardening experience, livestock, food preservation, renewable energy, water systems, and off-grid capability.

Section 6: Permaculture & Regenerative Practices — How do you build?

Permaculture training, soil health, water catchment, forest gardening, and regenerative agriculture principles.

Questions cover: permaculture design experience, soil management, water harvesting, polyculture, and ecological restoration.

Section 7: Social & Governance — How do you work with others?

Community living experience, conflict resolution, decision-making preferences, legal structure comfort, and family considerations.

Questions cover: prior community experience, conflict resolution skills, governance preferences, communication style, and family/children plans.

Section 8: Motivation & Values — Why are you doing this?

Core motivations, deal-breakers, time commitment, fallback plans, and alignment with the Window of Ascent thesis.

Questions cover: primary motivations, non-negotiables, time availability, backup plans, and philosophical alignment.
