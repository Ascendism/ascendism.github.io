After you join

Show up as yourself.
Take it one real step at a time.

There is no sales pitch, compatibility score, or expectation that anyone immediately share money, land, or a home.

01

Introduce yourself

Share what is drawing you here: your household, your timing, and the kind of future you are considering.

02

Find the conversations that matter

Talk about place, practical skills, family needs, boundaries, and what you can actually contribute.

03

Build trust before commitments

Get to know people through conversation and shared effort before any big decision about land or money.

Starting in Missouri

Built by Bobby Dean, for people across the country.

The first local effort begins in rural Missouri. The larger purpose is national: give people a direct way to find aligned others before trying to turn a big idea into a shared place. Member profiles and interview flow will grow from this foundation.
